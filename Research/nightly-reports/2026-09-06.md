### The baseline is older than yesterday's report said

I traced `events.explicit_pct` through every commit of `baselines.json`. **The pair 91.7 / n=1825
was written once, in `2489a18` (2026-07-31, "Round 1 (datum round): frames, emit, datum v1,
witness, fields, gate"), and has never been rewritten in 37 days.** Yesterday's report said "frozen
from 2026-08-28"; that is wrong — 2026-08-28 is simply a night on which it did not move, like every
other night. The ratchet only tightens metrics that improve, and this one never has, so it has sat
at day-one values while its siblings (`review.pct`, `grounded_direct`) were rewritten nightly.

So the refusal is a **baseline outliving its population**, measured: the floor was set against a
corpus of 1,825 events and is enforced tonight against 2,484 — 36.1% growth in the thing being
measured, with no re-measurement of the thing measuring it. The `recorded: 2026-07-31` stamp on
`baselines.json` is accurate for this field and stale for the ratcheted ones; it is a file-level
date over per-field ages.

The three options from yesterday are unchanged and still yours: split the metric by date-kind,
re-baseline against the current corpus, or make the comparison unrounded and re-baseline. A
