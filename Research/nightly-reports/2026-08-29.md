## 3. Finding — the novelty damper is spent for every rule that carries volume

`axes.novelty(k) = 0.15 + 0.85 × 0.5^k`, floor `NOVELTY_FLOOR = 0.15`. The docstring's table stops
at `k = 4 → 0.203` and then jumps to `k → ∞ → 0.150`. The gap between those two rows is where
every high-frequency rule now lives, and it is much narrower than the table implies:

```
k = 0   novelty 1.000000    566.7% above floor    100.00% of range live
k = 2   novelty 0.362500    141.7% above floor     25.00%
k = 4   novelty 0.203125     35.4% above floor      6.25%
k = 6   novelty 0.163281      8.9% above floor      1.56%
k = 7   novelty 0.156641      4.4% above floor      0.78%
k = 10  novelty 0.150830      0.6% above floor      0.10%
k = 15  novelty 0.150026      0.02% above floor     0.003%
```

Past **k ≈ 7** the damper is within 4.5% of the floor and every further repetition is
effectively free. Tonight, **all four applications came from rules at or past that point**:

```
ev-enforcement-action    k= 6.99   novelty 0.15670    4.47% above floor
ev-capital-commitment    k=15.15   novelty 0.15002    0.02% above floor
ev-safety-research       k= 6.63   novelty 0.15859    5.73% above floor
```

The evidence is direct and does not require the formula. Over the last nine days
`ev-capital-commitment` fired at `repeat_k` ranging **14.73 → 17.71** — a 20% swing — and its
magnitude was set **entirely by `spread`**:

```
spread 1.000  ->  mag 0.000450   (k = 15.54, 17.71, 16.95, 15.15)
spread 0.707  ->  mag 0.000318   (k = 16.28, 14.73)
spread 0.577  ->  mag 0.000260   (k = 14.98, 16.56)
```

0.000450 × 0.707 = 0.000318 and 0.000450 × 0.577 = 0.000260, to the digit. `repeat_k` moved the
result **not at all**. The rule has fired **45 times**; firing 45 lands exactly as hard as firing 15.

`spread = √n / n = 1/√n` is a **same-night** divisor — it splits a rule's budget among the
incidents it matches in one run. So the only functioning multiplicity damper cannot see across
nights, and the one that was supposed to (`novelty_k`, which reads prior nights) has spent its
whole dynamic range in the first seven firings.

**Rules whose damper is spent (k ≥ 6.8): 2 of 18 ever-fired — but they account for 64 of 143
lifetime applications (44.8%).** Including `ev-compute-buildout` at k = 5.24, the four
highest-volume rules are 107 of 143 (74.8%) and all sit in the flat tail.

This is the mechanism under the 2026-08-24 multiplicity finding, now measured. That night
established that every damper is keyed to the **rule** or the **axis**, never the **incident**.
Tonight adds the second half: for the rules that fire most, the rule-keyed damper is **numerically
dead**, so cross-night repetition is undamped in both dimensions at once.
