### 3.2 The Ox Alpha firing entered on a retraction, and said the timelines got shorter

`ev-benchmark-progress` fired on this, and wrote `T2 +1, T3 +1, T4 −1` — 0.436pp, 29.6% of the
night:

> Update on the unbranded Ox Alpha model covered this morning: as of August 23, 2026 no company
> has claimed it, **and the early benchmark claim has softened.** Developer Ben Davis, who first
> scored the model above 80% on ten DeepSWE tasks against 65% for Claude Fable 5 and 52% for
> GPT-5.6-sol, **has since run the full DeepSWE set and found Ox Alpha roughly level with
> GPT-5.6-sol mid rather than well ahead; no public leaderboard has posted a result.**

The event is a benchmark result being walked back. The rule read it as benchmark progress and
pulled capability timelines earlier.

Two things went wrong and they are separable:

**The trigger word is in the retraction.** `text_any` is `['benchmark*', 'state of the art',
'swe-bench', 'outperform*', 'surpass*', 'record score', 'frontier of']`. The phrase that matched
is *"the early benchmark claim has softened"* — the rule's own trigger vocabulary appears at full
strength in the sentence that reverses the finding, because a retraction is on-topic for the thing
it retracts. `text_none` is `['failed to', 'no better than', 'plateau*']`; the text says
"softened" and "roughly level with … rather than well ahead". Semantically these are `no better
than`. Lexically they are absent. This is the 2026-08-23 `raised` finding in new vocabulary, and
it generalises: **a `text_none` blacklist is a finite list against an open class, and the words
that name a subject are exactly the words a correction about that subject uses.**

**The quantified reversal is past the truncation point.** `events.json` stores `text` capped at
300 characters. The cut here lands at "has since r|un" — the clause carrying the corrected result
is entirely in the discarded tail. Corpus-wide: **1882 of 2287 events (82.3%) are truncated at
exactly 300 characters, 81.9% of those mid-word**; 97 of the 99 events with event dates since
2026-08-19. Of the 126 truncated events since 2026-08-18 whose full paragraphs I recovered from
the vault, **11 (8.7%) carry a hedge or reversal marker only in the discarded tail** — a keyword
proxy, so a floor and not a measurement.

Tonight the head window *did* contain the disconfirmation ("has softened") and the rule fired
anyway, so truncation is not what caused this firing. It is what removed the evidence that would
have made the firing obviously wrong. The two defects stack.
