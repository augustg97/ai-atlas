## 5. Findings

### 5a. The first undamped application in the model's history, and it turned S around

`ev-compute-constraint` had **never fired before** — it is 1 of 26 evidence rules and this is its
first entry in a 53-entry log. `novelty_k` counts prior incidents of the class, so k came in at
**0.000**, and `novelty(0) = NOVELTY_FLOOR + (1 − FLOOR) × 0.5^0 = 1.000` exactly. The application
therefore ran at the rule's full base magnitude, 0.003, with no novelty decay, no spread damping and
no corroboration bonus (one source). Every other application tonight ran at between ×0.156 and
×0.238 of base.

The app is honest about this — the evidence card displays **"novelty k (7-day half-life) 0 → ×1.0"**
and "asked +0.400pp · moved +0.400pp" on the nose, verified on the rendered page (§8). This is a
visible mechanism, not a hidden one.

What deserves August's attention is the consequence, which is structural rather than cosmetic:

- **S's entire night is this one story.** A producer-price-index print for electronic components,
  reported by one publisher, moved S3 ("supply constrained") +0.349pp and S2 ("diversified
  build-out", the mainline position) −0.284pp. That single application is 53% of the whole
  distribution's movement and drove the mainline joint to its largest fall since 08-01.
- **It reversed yesterday's direction and roughly doubled it.** On 08-10 two `ev-compute-buildout`
  applications, each spread-damped to 0.7071, moved S2 **+0.174pp**. Tonight one undamped
  first-of-class application moved it **−0.284pp**. The model's read of compute supply flipped on a
  macro price series after two named multi-gigawatt buildout stories had pushed it the other way.
- **Twelve of the 26 rules have still never fired**: `ev-export-retaliation`,
  `ev-frontier-release-gov-coordinated`, `ev-big-correction`, `ev-us-cn-agreement`,
  `ev-autonomous-rd-demonstrated`, `ev-unemployment-spike`, `ev-inference-tax-proposal`,
  `ev-distillation-attack`, `ev-sabotage-ops`, `ev-moratorium-movement`, `ev-state-bill-activity`,
  `ev-natsec-posture`. **Each is loaded with the same full-weight first shot.** Tonight is the first
  demonstration of what that means: the model's largest possible single-night move is reserved for
  whichever dormant rule wakes up first, and it will arrive as a single-source, never-before-reviewed
  classification. Several of those twelve (`ev-natsec-posture`, `ev-state-bill-activity`) map onto
  the largest standing residue clusters, so they are not unlikely to fire.

The novelty curve is doing what it was designed to do — "a DROUGHT-then-return carries weight". The
finding is that a *drought* and a *never* are treated identically, and there is no interaction
between novelty and corroboration: a one-source first-of-class event outweighs a well-corroborated
repeat by about 6×. Whether that is the intended trade is a design question, not a bug report, and
it is now on the record with a worked example.
