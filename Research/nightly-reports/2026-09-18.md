## Findings

### Incident identity stops at the edge of the night

`nightly_update.update` calls `resolve_incidents(fresh)` (nightly_update.py:429). That clusters
**tonight's fresh events only**. `select_fresh` keys on `fingerprint` = event date | section |
first 200 characters, so a reworded re-report of an already-applied development arriving in a
later digest is fresh, gets a new incident id, and is classified and applied again.
`INCIDENT_WINDOW_DAYS = 14` names a two-week identity window, but the haystack it is applied to is
one night. The design note (nightly_update.py:129-138) accepts under-merging "which the novelty
floor now handles gracefully". But `novelty_k` counts prior incidents *of the class*, so it has no
notion of identity. Tonight the repeat got 80% of the first application's magnitude
(0.00045 vs 0.00056).

corpus-wide (the resolver's own `URL_DF_MAX` test for a non-digest link):
