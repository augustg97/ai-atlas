## Findings

**1 — A sub-axis's identity is a truncation of its name, and the dedup guard turns the collision
into silence.** `schema_review()` builds the key as
`"%s.watch-%s" % (axis, section.lower().split(" ")[0].strip(".,&"))` — the axis letter plus the
**first word only** of the section name. The 13 sections in `SECTION_AXIS` collapse to **12 keys**:

```
C.watch-ai  <-  "AI Litigation, Liability & Enforcement"
            <-  "AI Standards & Safety Frameworks"
```

Both map to axis C and both generate `C.watch-ai`. Whichever clusters first claims the key; the
`if key in existing: continue` guard — which is correct on its own, and exists so the review does
not re-propose the same sub-axis every Monday forever — then suppresses the other **permanently and
silently**. No error, no log line, no second addition ever. Latent tonight: neither section has
reached the 12-event threshold, so nothing wrong is published yet. Same family as the window-label
defect: **an identity built from a truncated name, plus a correct de-duplicator, equals a thing
that can never be said twice.** Key off the full section name (or an explicit section→key map), and
have the guard distinguish "already added" from "key taken by a different section".

**2 — The night the artefact doesn't move is a measurement.** `exemplars.json` reproduced
byte-for-byte across a night that moved 1.05 pp of mass over five axes. That is not a stall — the
file was written with the other ten — it is the resolution of the narrative layer being measured for
free. The letters of all 120 lines are stable to perturbations of this size. Worth recording as the
scale at which the published surface is insensitive; if a future night moves comparable mass **and**
exemplars changes, that is a boundary crossing worth a look.

**3 — The published marginals and the ensemble have drifted further apart.** `marginals.json.today`
publishes uncoupled priors while the 2,000-line ensemble is coupled. Tonight the **maximum gap is
8.93 pp at S.S1** (23.62% published vs 32.55% empirical), overtaking K.K1 (51.48% vs 43.05%,
8.43 pp) which held the maximum when this was last measured. **37 of 61 positions differ by more
than 1 pp**; mean |gap| 2.08 pp. `activeMarginals()` (`web/index.html:716`) still silently swaps
prior for empirical once a condition is active, so the same axis reads two different numbers
depending on UI state.
