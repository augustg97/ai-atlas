stale, and it aborted the chain before any real work ran.

**derived from `axes.REGISTRY`** and asserts round-trip normalisation on *every* axis. The registry
is designed to grow — C5 by hand, sub-axes by the Monday review — so a hardcoded position list was
guaranteed to break the chain again on the next addition. It can no longer go stale.
Swept the other four engine modules for the same pattern: none.
