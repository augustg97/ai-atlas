## 4. The finding: an update's direction is a property of the rule, never of the event

This has been reported for three mornings as a fault in one rule. It is not. Read off
`axes.EVIDENCE_RULES` directly: **all 26 rules carry a fixed `nudge` dict**, and across the 40
applications now served, every rule has produced exactly one direction vector (the apparent variants
are float rounding at the fourth decimal — `−0.4998` against `−0.5001`). A rule matches on **event
type**; nothing in the mechanism can read a **finding's direction**. So a rule fires the same way on
the result and on its refutation.

Three of tonight's eight are the consequence.

**`ev-capital-commitment` on a $15 billion loss.** The record, verbatim:

> Update on the Situational Awareness unwind: Jane Street told employees in a note dated August 14,
> 2026 that it took a **$15 billion hit** in July 2026 from its investment in the AI-focused hedge
> fund and from other tech positions, **its first negative month of trading revenue since 2016** …

Applied: `E.E1 +0.00030` ("the boom holds: revenue growth validates the capex"), `E.E3 −0.00015`
("deflates hard, capex-led"). An AI-linked position being unwound at a fifteen-billion-dollar loss —
the largest single financial number in the night's record — was recorded as evidence that the boom is
holding, and as evidence **against** a hard deflation.

**`ev-safety-research` on a risk-estimate upgrade.** Verbatim:

> Anthropic published a redacted risk report on August 14, 2026 **raising its broad estimate of the
> risk of misalignment in high-stakes situations to "low" from "very low,"** citing recent
> cybersecurity incidents, and disclosing an unreleased internal model it calls Model 2 …

Applied: `A.A3 +0.00043` ("alignment proves tractable with sustained effort"), `A.A1 −0.00021`
("misalignment that evades detection"). The rule's own source comment states the intent — *"Research
output is weak evidence the problem is tractable"* — and that intent is exactly the defect: the act
of publishing is treated as the evidence, and what was published is not read. **This is the same item
that fired the same rule the same way in yesterday's report.** It is now the 12th
`ev-safety-research` application, and A1 has been pushed **down 20 times and up twice** in the
model's lifetime.

**`ev-compute-buildout` on a paper about the costs of build-out.** Verbatim:

> Alex Hsu and Hala Moussawi of the Georgia Institute of Technology's Scheller College of Business
> posted a 46-page working paper to SSRN on August 7, 2026, dated July 29, finding that **the fiscal
> costs of data centers fall on municipalities neighboring the host rather than on the host itself.**

Applied: `S.S2 +0.00086` ("diversified build-out: the capex wave lands widely"), `S.S3 −0.00043`
("constrained: controls + energy"). Two errors compounded. First it is the **commentary-framing trap
README already names** — a *paper about* build-out matched as an *instance of* build-out, on
`data center*`. Second, its finding is an uncompensated-externality result, which is a mechanism of
**local resistance** — evidence toward constraint and toward populist backlash, not toward a
build-out landing smoothly.

The fourth, De Marzo et al. on majority-following in LLM agent groups, is thin rather than inverted:
a descriptive coordination result, arguably understanding gained, arguably an unpredictability
finding. It was applied as tractability. I would not call it wrong; I would call it unreadable by
this mechanism either way.

**Zero contested readings, for a fifth day, is not agreement.** Each of the eight was matched by one
rule. With every rule carrying a single fixed vector, two rules can only disagree by both matching —
which requires overlapping text gates, not opposing readings of the same text. The `contested`
field cannot detect the failure mode above, by construction.
